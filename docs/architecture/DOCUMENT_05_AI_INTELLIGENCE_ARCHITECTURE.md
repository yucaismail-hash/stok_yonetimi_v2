# 1. PURPOSE

Bu doküman;

# Revision — Product Architecture Phase 1

- **Revision date:** 2026-08-06
- **Revision status:** BINDING
- **ADR reference:** ADR-020

Standalone Analysis AI mode enables Learning and disables Decision Intelligence. AI explains only the selected analysis result, producing an analysis-level AI Explanation Artifact.

Business Workflow AI mode enables Learning and Decision Intelligence. Decision Intelligence evaluates deterministic outputs and produces the final operational decision as a Dynamic Operational Plan; the final AI Artifact explains that operational decision.

Learning does not retroactively change completed deterministic results. It improves future behaviour and parameter decisions and may learn from future ERP/API actual actions. ERP/API feedback is V2 / future unless already implemented.

Deterministic engines calculate. AI learns, optimizes, evaluates, decides, and explains; it cannot replace deterministic analytical engines.

# Revision — Capability Design Principles

- **Revision date:** 2026-08-06
- **Revision status:** BINDING
- **Scope:** Forecast, Safety Stock, Simulation, Backtest, Supplier, Learning, Decision Intelligence, and LLM-based explanation design. This append introduces no implementation or method inventory.

## 1. Scientific Foundation

New analytical methods and capability algorithms must use peer-reviewed or academically established methods, widely accepted industrial or statistical standards, openly verifiable deterministic techniques, or explicitly approved experiments isolated from canonical production results. An experiment cannot become the sole canonical production method without validation criteria, Backtest comparison with a baseline, explainable failure behavior, and explicit approval. Simple methods remain valid when evidence shows they are suitable, stable, or explainable.

## 2. Deterministic First

Deterministic calculation owns the analytical result. AI may optimize bounded parameters, learn historical performance, rank candidate methods, support decisions, explain results, identify patterns, and recommend validated alternatives. AI must not replace calculation, invent unverified numerical results, bypass mathematical constraints, rewrite completed deterministic outputs, or present invalid results as valid.

**Required direction:** Deterministic Analysis → Learning / Optimization → Decision Support → Explanation.

## 3. Capability Continuity

A capability must publish validated downstream information as well as its final number. A Forecast information package may include forecast values, confidence, residuals, error metrics, trend, seasonality, demand pattern, uncertainty, model identity/version, and data-quality warnings. These are examples only, not implementation authorization.

Every capability output contract distinguishes final user-facing output, machine-consumable downstream information, validation evidence, warnings/uncertainty, and provenance/version. Transfer uses explicit versioned contracts or approved adapters, never undocumented dictionary conventions.

## 4. Capability Cooperation

Capabilities are evaluated against upstream results, downstream consumers, Learning, Decision Intelligence, Product Level, and degradation behavior. Forecast may support Safety Stock, Simulation, Backtest, Pattern Intelligence, and Decision Intelligence. Safety Stock may support Simulation, Backtest, Dynamic Inventory Plan, and Supplier Allocation enrichment. Simulation and Backtest contribute risk and validation evidence rather than disconnected reports.

Execution order does not prove a data dependency. Each relationship is explicitly classified as required data dependency, execution-order dependency, optional enrichment dependency, unsupported, or adapter required.

## 5. Multi-Method Capability Design and Method Selection

One capability may contain multiple academically or industrially established internal candidate methods; they do not automatically become separate product modules or Business Workflows. Safety Stock candidates may include Classical Safety Stock, Croston-based methods, Syntetos-Boylan Approximation, bootstrapping, approved ML, and Hybrid/Ensemble approaches. Forecast candidates may include naive/seasonal-naive baselines, moving average, exponential smoothing, Holt/Holt-Winters, intermittent-demand methods, ARIMA/SARIMA, approved ML, and Hybrid/Ensemble approaches. The actual production inventory remains implementation evidence.

Stokonomi must not depend on one universal SKU method. Candidate selection may consider data volume/quality, demand pattern, intermittency, trend, seasonality, lead time, Backtest, Simulation risk, service level, stockout/overstock cost, stability, Company Learning, and Product Intelligence evidence. Product language uses “most suitable,” “recommended according to Backtest,” or “most balanced,” not absolute “best method” claims.

## 6. Hybrid and Ensemble Governance

Hybrid/Ensemble output is composed only from validated candidate methods. AI may optimize weights only within approved bounds. Governance records candidate identity/version, weights/bounds, selection evidence, Backtest and applicable Simulation evidence, fallback, and explanation. AI must not independently generate unrestricted forecast or stock values. Hybrid does not win by default; simpler, stable, explainable, better-validated methods may be selected.

## 7. Backtest, Simulation, and Learning

Where ADR-020 requires them, Backtest and Simulation are mandatory Business Workflow evidence: Backtest evaluates historical behavior and Simulation evaluates uncertainty/future risk. They may provide error, bias, service-level realization, stockout probability, overstock behavior, CVaR, tail risk, stability, and scenario sensitivity. Their validated outputs remain available to Learning, method selection, Decision Intelligence, and explanation; they do not overwrite deterministic results.

Learning never retroactively changes a completed deterministic result. It improves future behavior through method/parameter performance, company and supplier feedback, pattern change, and future ERP/API or user feedback when implemented. Learning preserves tenant isolation, provenance, method/model version, confidence, validation history, source, and timestamp. Optional Learning input never blocks core deterministic analysis.

## 8. Decision Intelligence and Explainability

Decision Intelligence performs no underlying analytical calculation. It evaluates validated applicable capability outputs, including Forecast, Safety Stock, Simulation, Backtest, Supplier, Learning, Pattern Intelligence, and approved External Intelligence-derived parameters. It must not consume failed/invalid results, fabricated placeholders, unversioned unknown payloads, or cross-company data. It produces the final Business Workflow decision, not an underlying calculation.

Method recommendations and AI-supported decisions explain evaluated methods, selection reasons, data characteristics, Backtest/Simulation influence, confidence/uncertainty, limitations, unavailable optional data, and fallback use. Normal user messages do not expose internal runtime terminology and answer: what was calculated, which method was recommended, why, reliability, and user action.

## 9. Graceful Degradation and Product-Level Consistency

Missing optional supplier data, External Intelligence, ML evidence, history, or Learning enrichment must not unnecessarily block core analysis. The system uses approved fallback and reduced confidence where applicable, and explicitly explains unavailable enrichment without pretending it was used.

Standalone Analysis executes one selected capability, may evaluate multiple internal methods, follows approved Learning rules, excludes Decision Intelligence, and produces an analysis-level AI Explanation Artifact. Business Workflow orchestrates required ordered capabilities, Learning, Decision Intelligence, a Dynamic Operational Plan, and final AI Artifact. No capability absorbs another Product Level or layer responsibility.

## 10. Continuous Evolution and LLM Boundary

New methods require version identity, baseline comparison, Backtest evidence, regression testing, fallback, explicit output contract, compatibility, runtime-cost, and explainability assessment. Company Learning may improve future selection but cannot create uncontrolled self-modification.

LLMs may explain validated results, summarize findings, translate validated context to user language, support structured narratives, identify review inconsistencies, and use validated multi-capability context. They must not fabricate missing results, silently fill failed outputs, replace deterministic capabilities, consume invalid results as successful, change stored deterministic outputs, mix tenant data, or unnecessarily expose confidential/decrypted raw data. Every LLM statement is traceable to validated analytical or Learning evidence.

## 11. Capability Continuity Gate

Before a new or materially changed capability is approved, its design answers:

1. Validated inputs consumed.
2. Deterministic result produced.
3. Downstream information published and consumers.
4. Required versus order-only dependencies.
5. Optional-input degradation and fallback.
6. Backtest and uncertainty evidence.
7. Learning retention and Decision Intelligence input.
8. User explanation method.
9. Runtime/cost impact.
10. Academic or industrial support.

A capability is not production-ready while these relationships remain undefined.

## Phase 2E Capability Dataflow Contract

Validated upstream evidence is binding. Only same-company, validated, compatible-version RuntimeResultReferences may populate `CapabilityExecutionRequest.upstream_results`; each transfer records result-reference provenance.

Business Simulation consumes validated Forecast and Safety Stock evidence and must not invoke either analytical engine again. Standalone Simulation may use explicit historical and existing-policy fallback, marked with provenance. Backtest historical rolling recalculation remains required; Business Workflow uses `VALIDATE_SELECTED` and may not reselect a strategy, while Standalone `COMPARE_CANDIDATES` remains available.
